Personal Finance Tool

A simple, private personal finance tool that lives entirely in your browser — budget planning, loan payoff projections, and more. No accounts, no subscriptions, no data sharing.

How It Works
📅
Set Your Budget
Go to Monthly Budget and enter how much you plan to spend in each category per month. Use ⚡ Auto to pre-fill from past transactions, or → to copy a value forward to all remaining months.
💳
Log Transactions
Add transactions manually or upload a CSV from your bank. Map your bank's category column to auto-fill labels, or assign categories in the review step before importing.
⚖️
Compare Budget vs Actuals
See how your actual spending compares to your plan for any month — by category, section, and total. Toggle Hide Empty Rows to focus only on categories with activity.
✦
Review Your Summary
The Summary tab shows year-to-date KPIs vs your YTD budget, plus 7 charts: income vs expenses, spending mix donut, savings rate, cumulative cash flow, top overspend categories, and a spending heatmap.
Key Features
📥 Bank CSV Import
Import transactions straight from your bank's CSV export. Duplicates are detected automatically, and you review everything before it's saved.
🏛️ Loan Planner
Track every loan, see your debt-free date, and test how extra monthly payments shrink your payoff timeline and total interest.
→ Fill Forward
Hover over any budget cell and click → to copy that value forward to all remaining months — no more typing the same number 12 times.
🔍 Smart Filtering
Filter transactions by month, category type, or search by description. Hide empty rows on Budget vs Actuals to cut the noise.
⬇ Export / ⬆ Import
Export all your data as a JSON file to back it up or transfer to another computer. Import restores everything instantly.
🌙 Dark Mode
Toggle dark mode from the bottom of the sidebar. Your preference is saved automatically.
🔒 100% Private
Everything stays in your browser's local storage. No data ever leaves your device. No login or account required.

💡 Tips for Getting Started
  1. Set your year — Use the Year field at the bottom of the sidebar to make sure you're budgeting for the right year.
  2. Enter your income first — Start in Monthly Budget → INCOME so you know what you have to work with.
  3. Import your bank CSV — Download a transaction export from your bank, then click ⬆ Import CSV on the Transactions tab. Map the Category column if your bank already labels transactions.
  4. Use ⚡ Auto to set budgets — Once you have transactions, click Auto on any month column to pre-fill budgets from your actual spending history.
  5. Export regularly — Click ⬇ Export in the sidebar to save a backup of all your data. Store it in Google Drive or email it to yourself.
  6. Sharing with friends & family — Send this HTML file to anyone. They open it in their browser and start fresh. Use Export/Import to share your actual budget data with a partner.

Welcome to Budget Planner

A personal finance tool to help you plan your budget, track spending, and understand where your money goes — all in one place, privately stored on your device.

How it works
📅
1. Set your budget
Go to Monthly Budget and enter how much you plan to spend in each category for each month. Use ⚡ Auto to pre-fill from past transactions, or → to copy a value across future months.
💳
2. Log transactions
Add transactions manually or import a CSV from your bank — you review and confirm everything before importing. Edit any row after the fact with the ✏ button.
📊
3. Track progress
Budget vs. Actuals shows how spending compares to your plan. The Summary tab gives you a full-year overview with 7 interactive charts, YTD metrics, and a spending heatmap.
Feature guide
📅 Monthly Budget
  • ›Enter budget amounts for each category and month in the scrollable grid
  • ›Click ⚡ Auto on any month column to auto-fill from your transaction history
  • ›Hover a cell and click → to copy that value forward to all remaining months in one click
  • ›The Category column and month headers stay frozen as you scroll right
💳 Transactions
  • ›Add individual transactions with the form at the top of the page
  • ›Import a full bank CSV — map your columns and review everything before it saves
  • ›If your CSV has a Category column, map it and matching rows are labeled automatically
  • ›Click ✏ on any transaction to edit it — category, amount, date, and notes
  • ›Use the filter bar to search by description, filter by category type or section, and sort
  • ›Download all transactions as a CSV any time with ⇩ Download CSV
⚖️ Budget vs. Actuals
  • ›Select a month from the dropdown to compare your budget against actual spending
  • ›Toggle Hide empty rows to focus only on categories with data
  • ›Categories with spending but no budget are flagged ✘ No Budget Set in red
  • ›Each row shows variance, % of budget used, a progress bar, and an on-track/over-budget status
✦ Summary
  • ›YTD KPI cards compare your actual income, expenses, savings, and net against your budget for the same months
  • ›7 interactive charts: monthly trend, savings rate, spending mix donut, section BvA, cumulative net, top overspend, and a spending heatmap
  • ›Hover over any chart or heatmap cell for detailed breakdowns including top transactions
⇩ Export & ⇧ Import
  • ›Use ⇩ Export in the sidebar to save all budgets and transactions to a JSON file
  • ›Use ⇧ Import on another computer to load that file and pick up exactly where you left off
  • ›This is how you sync your data across devices — just carry the JSON file
💡 Tips & good to know
›All data is stored privately in your browser — nothing leaves your device
›Negative transaction amounts are supported and offset category totals
›Use the Year field in the sidebar to switch between budget years
›Toggle Dark mode at the bottom of the sidebar anytime
›Savings categories count as expenses in net cash flow but are shown separately
›Hover the ⓘ icon on Net Cash Flow rows to see the formula
